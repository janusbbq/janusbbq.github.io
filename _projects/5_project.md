**State Grid Zhejiang Hangzhou Fuyang Power Supply Co. · Substation Maintenance Technician · Aug 2010 – Dec 2012**

## 110kV substation construction

These projects were the Xintong (Sep 2011 – Apr 2012), Chating (Apr – Jun 2012), and Dongzhou Phase II (Sep – Dec 2012) substations. My work on them included:

- installing 50MVA main transformers, including assembly, oil filling, and testing;
- installing 10kV switchgear lineups (21 panels per expansion), bus bridges, and wall bushings;
- installing 110kV CTs, VTs, surge arresters, disconnect switches, post insulators, and tubular bus;
- erecting structures and lightning masts, and installing grounding;
- laying and terminating secondary cables from the 10kV equipment to the control room.

## 35kV projects

- **Jingu substation.** A 20MVA 35/10.5kV substation with 7 feeders and hybrid static var compensation with harmonic filtering. I installed the communication and metering devices and the telemetry database on an XJ CBZ-8000 integrated automation system.
- **Shengcun DC system retrofit.** Installed a new DC panel, reconfigured the battery bank, and cut the feeder loads over from both old DC buses.

## Testing and commissioning

- Tested and commissioned the arc-suppression coil and capacitor bank systems.
- Performed secondary circuit checks.
- Completed SCADA point-to-point tests (telemetry, telesignaling, telecontrol) with the district and city dispatch centers.

## Project support and maintenance

- Supported four 35–110kV substation and industrial-customer projects (Sep 2010 – Jun 2011). The work covered bid technical specifications, equipment and material order lists, contract review and technical briefings, receiving inspections, site coordination, and as-built documents and drawings for handover.
- Maintained and repaired substation equipment. This included HV/MV/LV electrical testing, transformer oil sampling, metering installation, and maintenance records.
- Named Safety Production Advanced Individual (2013).
