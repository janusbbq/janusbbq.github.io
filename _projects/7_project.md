**State Grid Zhejiang Hangzhou Fuyang Power Supply Co. · Electronic Information Technician · Jan – Nov 2015**

_10kV distribution network, IoT, sensors, cloud platform, ARM, RESTful APIs_

Condensation and flashover are a common cause of equipment failure in 10kV residential switching stations, and finding the conditions that lead to them used to mean sending crews out on manual patrols. This project replaced most of those patrols with continuous remote monitoring.

- Designed and implemented an IoT-based cloud platform for real-time monitoring of SF6 gas, temperature, humidity, smoke, and oxygen levels in 10kV residential switching stations.
- Built ARM-based data terminals and RESTful APIs for remote diagnostics and intelligent alerting.
- Enabled 24/7 remote inspection. This reduced manual patrols by over 70% and prevented up to 40% of the failures caused by condensation and flashover, improving equipment safety and reducing maintenance costs.
