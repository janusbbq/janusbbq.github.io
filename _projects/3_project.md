This research direction studies analytical methods for understanding operating limits, vulnerability, and resilience in modern power systems. The work connects traditional power-system analysis with optimization and AI-assisted workflows, with an emphasis on tools that remain interpretable to engineers.

Topics include:

- voltage stability assessment under stressed operating conditions;
- optimization-based screening of weak operating points;
- resilience-oriented analysis of contingencies and recovery options;
- clear visualization and reporting for decision support.
