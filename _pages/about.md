I am Jian Han, a power systems researcher working at the intersection of electric power engineering, optimization, and artificial intelligence. My work focuses on AI-enabled power-system analysis, voltage stability assessment, grid resilience, and decision-support tools for modern power networks.

Before my current research work, I gained industry experience at State Grid Corporation of China (SGCC), where I worked close to practical power-system operation and planning problems. That experience continues to shape my interest in methods that are not only technically rigorous, but also usable in real grid-analysis workflows.

## Research Interests

- AI-enabled power-system analysis and automation
- Optimization for power system operation, planning, and stability assessment
- Voltage stability, PV/QV curve analysis, and weak-grid diagnosis
- Resilience analysis for transmission and distribution networks
- Large language model agents for engineering workflows and grid studies

## Current Directions

I am currently building research and software prototypes around LLM-assisted automated modeling, voltage stability analysis, and interpretable decision support for power-system engineers. Selected projects are listed on the [projects](/projects/) page, and publication entries will be updated as manuscripts become public.
